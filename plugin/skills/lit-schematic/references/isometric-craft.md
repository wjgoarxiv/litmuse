# Isometric and cutaway craft

Camera: dimetric view from front-left-above, elevation near 30 degrees, azimuth near 45.
Ground plane carries a soft grid; the machine sits centred with clear air around it.

Solids: turned parts through `lathe` (shells, heads, nozzles, shafts), flat parts through
`box` and `prism` (skids, saddles, frames, brackets), routes through `pipe` with bends
following the same router as piping sheets. Paint far-to-near so near faces cover far ones.

Tones follow the `MATERIAL` palette: painted steel, stainless, copper, brass, cast iron,
concrete, glass. Shade curved bodies in smooth bands along the surface. Grey serves quiet or
monochrome figures requested as such.

Parts list: at least 40 real parts per machine, each with a material tone, grouped as ground
(pad, skid, feet), structure (saddles, legs, frame), body (shell, heads, flanges), internals
(tubes, baffles, impeller, coil, packing, liquid), connections (nozzles, bolts, pipes), and
instruments (gauges, thermowells, transmitters). Seat every part on another part.

Windows: cut openings reveal internals with `into`; keep shell around nozzles and supports.
Callouts sit in columns beside the art with leaders ending in dots on the named part; two
dots sharing a small region split into separate leaders.

Stage: cap the composition at a readable height for the medium. Geometry lives in a scene
function so the static view, the interactive page, and the motion loop share one source.
